# Before implementation: visual diagnosis

Baseline captured from the actual deployed game at fixed spawn (180,20,195), heading PI, default camera 16.8 m / 6 m / 65 degrees. See before-desktop.png and before-desktop.json. The earlier supplied target concept is available; the two newly described TARGET/CURRENT attachments were not present in this turn, so no claim is made to have inspected a new iPhone image.

1. Terrain has relief numerically, but the default view looks over a very broad base clearing and tiny rolling waves. Primary sine-based dunes create regular ridges rather than legible hillsides and valleys. The Octopod clearance must be preserved, but its surroundings need an intentional 200 m composition with substantial side slopes and silhouettes.
2. Sand repeats 20 normal-map cycles in each 8 m tile: approximately 0.4 m parallel corrugations, with normal strength 0.8. Oblique viewing turns these into dominant repetitive diagonal stripes. The per-fragment hash grain is unfiltered and adds aliasing. Fine grain and lower-amplitude irregular ripples should replace this, with mipmapped texture filtering.
3. The dense modular reef is around the original origin, far from the Octopod spawn (180,195). The opening panorama uses tiny disconnected legacy pockets. Increasing map-wide random counts would not solve the initial visual composition.
4. Dark vertex colors multiply already shaded rock textures. Mobile disables all real-time shadows and reduces vegetation; it also uses low LODs even near the camera. The ambient fill/directional ratio plus dark rock albedo creates flat lit sand alongside very dark props. Contact shading exists on sand but not enough structural color separation.
5. FogExp2 density 0.0072 gives only about 12% visibility at 200 m, blending distant terrain into uniform cyan. Need a layered backdrop and a longer readable middle distance, not simply denser fog.
6. The camera now uses a reasonable three-length framing. Preserve it initially so visual comparisons are valid. The GUP-B has no environment reflection source; improve its materials and add a small shared stylized environment map, without changing geometry or controls.

Plan: first correct coherent terrain shaping and sand scale, capture; then author new low-cost rock modules in Blender and compose a showcase around the existing opening valley, capture; then tune shared rock/coral shading, sunlight/fog and GUP-B reflections, capture. Use the same spawn/camera for all opening comparisons. Extend coherent terrain/material/shading globally, with a smooth spatial blend and sparse reusable clusters beyond the showcase, rather than copying the dense region everywhere.
