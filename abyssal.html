This is the original renderer and environment, with first-person swimming. GUP driving is available in the separate adapted prototype.

Desktop: Dive in, then mouse to look; WASD swim, E/Q rise/dive, Shift faster, 1–6 viewpoints, Esc menu. Use Low / Medium / High in its menu and H for performance stats.

Touch: hold one finger and slide to swim/steer; second finger goes faster. Tap ≡ for the menu. Touch support is experimental.

Requires WebGPU and a compatible GPU/browser. Chrome or Edge on desktop is recommended. There is no automatic fallback.
